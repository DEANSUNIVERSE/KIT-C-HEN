How long does an architectural kitchen build take?
Our signature transformations are delivered within 8 to 12 weeks once fabrication materials arrive on-site, minimizing household downtime.
Are permits and regulatory documentation managed?
Yes. Our studio navigates local municipal building codes, architectural variances, and board approvals directly.
What separates the Standard, Premium, and Luxury tiers?
Every tier includes complete structural demolition and installation. Luxury incorporates custom-milled cabinetry, monolithic imported marble, and fully integrated smart appliance suites.
